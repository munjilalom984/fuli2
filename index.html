Privacy Policy

Whiteboard App

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 • Version 1.0

Summary: Whiteboard is built with a strict local-first, privacy-by-design architecture. Your drawings, notes, shapes, and brainstorms remain 100% on your personal device. We do not collect, track, or share your personal data.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Whiteboard ("we", "our", or "the app") treats user data and information when you use our application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent and privacy-friendly practices.

2. Information Collection & Storage

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • Drawings, Notes & Board Content: All strokes, sketches, geometric shapes, sticky notes, and text elements you create are saved directly to your device's internal SQLite database (via Android Room). None of your board content is uploaded to any cloud server or third-party service.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need an account, email address, password, or login to use the app.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The app does not bundle tracking SDKs, device fingerprinting, advertising identifiers, or analytics libraries.

3. Device Permissions & File Sharing

The app operates on the principle of least privilege:

  • Export & Sharing: When you choose to export your whiteboard canvas as a PNG image, the image is rendered locally and saved temporarily in the app's secure cache directory. The file is shared strictly via Android's secure FileProvider directly to the app you select (e.g., messaging, email, or drive).
  • No Broad Storage Permissions: The app does not request or require broad storage access (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).
  • No Network Telemetry: The app does not transmit user data across the Internet.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full ownership and control over all content created in Whiteboard:

  • You can delete individual whiteboards, sticky notes, or clear entire canvases at any time inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage from Android Settings permanently removes all saved boards, drawings, and app cache from your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Whiteboard does not share or sell your data to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. When you utilize the native Android share sheet to export a whiteboard, the handling of the exported PNG image is governed by the privacy policy of the specific destination app you choose.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Whiteboard does not collect personal information of any kind, our application is safe for all audiences, including children under 13 years of age, in compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and the EU GDPR.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
